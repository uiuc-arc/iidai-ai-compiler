AI Compiler Thrust

Compilation systems for emerging AI models on GPUs, accelerators, and the rest of the heterogeneous compute stack.

Overview

The AI Compiler Thrust brings together UIUC and IBM researchers to develop the systems for compilation of emerging AI models on modern computational platforms. AI compilers bridge heterogeneous compute ecosystem, including GPUs and AI accelerators, and high-level AI applications’ descriptions, with the goal to reduce energy consumption, latency, and monetary cost for cloud infrastructure.

Repositories

    uiuc-arc/neptune
